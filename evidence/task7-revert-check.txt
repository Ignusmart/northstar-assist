Northstar's hybrid work policy is outlined in the **Company Policies Handbook**. The key elements of the hybrid work policy are:

- **Eligibility**: Northstar offers a hybrid work arrangement for eligible positions.
- **Remote Work Days**: Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.
- **In-Office Days**: Minimum 2 days per week in-office (typically Tuesday-Thursday).
- **Coordination**: Remote work days should be coordinated with team/manager.
- **Fully Remote Positions**: Fully remote positions are available for certain roles (as specified in job description).
